# Hawaii

Synthetic Hawaii, 60 Hz balanced three-phase EMT. 37 AC buses, 77 lines, 12 transformers, 30 synchronous machines and nine grid-following plants.
Each synchronous machine has an iron-loss resistance of 300 pu on its own rating.

## Development

The [state](./Hawaii.state.json) gives the initial operating point. The case is
used by the [Hawaii validation study](../../../examples/EMT/Validation/Hawaii/README.md).
