Every figure: ParaEMT on top, GridKit in the middle, GridKit − ParaEMT on the bottom. All three panels use identical x/y scales. Three-phase signals stay together. Speed is shown as deviation from synchronous speed in pu. Errors use common output times without interpolation or phase alignment.

Trip caveat: GridKit models an ideal terminal opening. ParaEMT freezes generator 1 electrical history but continues its state kernel. Its post-trip generator 1 response and trip spike cannot validate the disconnected GridKit machine.
