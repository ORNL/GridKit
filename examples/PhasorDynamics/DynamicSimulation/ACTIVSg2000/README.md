# ACTIVSg2000 Line Outage

This study uses the [ACTIVSg2000 case](../../../../cases/PhasorDynamics/ACTIVSg2000/README.md)
and its separate operating-state file.

At $t=1$ second, circuit `branch_5317_5260_1` between buses 5317 and 5260
opens and remains out of service. The buses remain connected through the
surrounding meshed network. The simulation runs until $t=10$ seconds.
